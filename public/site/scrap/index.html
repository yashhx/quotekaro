Seedhi baat

Jo sab puchte hain

Mera munshi register me likhta hai. Wahi kaafi nahi?

The register records — it never compares. It will faithfully note every entry while 17 tonnes walk out, because paper cannot subtract trucks from stock or ring an alarm. TrackRakho does both, and your munshi can be the one typing the entries.

Tally to accountant ke paas hai. Kaise connect hoga?

A small connector program runs on the Tally computer — a 10-minute one-time setup. Your accountant changes nothing. The numbers appear on your phone, read-only, safe.

Kya naya kanta ya hardware lagega?

Nothing. Your existing weighbridge, your existing phone. The app is the discipline, not the machinery.

Ghata dikha to kya proof milega?

Every kanta check is logged with date, book quantity, weighed quantity, and the difference. A dated trail of when stock last matched — exactly what you need for a serious conversation.
