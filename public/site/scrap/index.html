The platform

Six capabilities. One system of record.

Everything a trading operation runs on — connected, so every entry updates the whole picture.

⚖️

Inventory & stock control

Live stock by material in metric tonnes. Every inward entry and every dispatch updates the book quantity automatically.

Weekly verification against physical stock flags discrepancies immediately — the control that prevents slow, unnoticed inventory loss and theft.
🚚

Fleet & dispatch board

Every truck, every trip: material, weighbridge weight, destination and reference number. See which vehicles are out and what they carry, from anywhere.

📒

Tally integration

A read-only connector on your accountant's computer brings receivables, payables and sales vouchers — with voucher numbers and references — to your phone. Nothing changes in Tally.

💰

Receivables & payables

Party-wise outstanding balances, always current. Every deal card also shows that party's pending balance, so you negotiate fully informed.

🗓️

Dispatch planning

An intelligent planner weighs delivery deadlines, waiting time, remaining quantity and outstanding payments — and recommends the optimal dispatch order, with every reason shown.

📋

Deal pipeline

Every enquiry and deal — pending, won, lost — with follow-up reminders and one-tap WhatsApp messages. Incoming WhatsApp enquiries can be logged in a single tap.

See it in action

A day in the app, as you scroll.

This is the actual interface — follow one working day from morning overview to evening reconciliation.

STEP 01 — 9:00 AM

Open the day with the full picture

Pending deal value, this month's dispatches, receivables and fleet status — the numbers that matter, before your first phone call. No reports to ask for; it's simply there.

STEP 02 — 11:30 AM

A truck is loaded and dispatched

At the weighbridge, your operator logs the trip in under a minute: truck, material, weight, destination, bill reference. The truck now shows as out on the board — visible to you wherever you are.

STEP 03 — SAME MOMENT

Inventory updates itself

That dispatch is automatically deducted from MS / Iron stock. Book quantity, weekly inflow and outflow stay current per material — no separate stock register, no evening reconciliation.

STEP 04 — SATURDAY

Weekly stock verification

Weigh a material and enter one number. The app compares it with the book quantity and flags any variance the same day — with a dated record. Slow inventory losses that once hid for months are caught in a week.

STEP 05 — PLANNING

Collections and the next dispatch

Party-wise receivables arrive from Tally, and the delivery planner recommends where the next truck should go — considering deadlines, waiting time and pending payments, with its reasoning shown in full.

Common questions

Straight answers

Our records are in Tally with our accountant. How does this fit?

A small read-only connector runs on the Tally computer — a ten-minute, one-time setup. Your accountant's workflow doesn't change at all; the relevant numbers simply appear on your phone.

Do we need new weighbridge hardware or sensors?

No. Your existing weighbridge and existing phones are enough. TrackRakho is the discipline layer on top of the equipment you already own.

How does stock verification actually prevent losses?

The system always knows the book quantity per material: previous stock plus inward entries minus dispatches. When a weekly physical check disagrees with the book, the variance is flagged and recorded with a date. Losses that traditionally hide for months become visible within the week — with a documented trail.

Can our staff use it?

Yes — it's designed for the yard, not for an office. Large text, plain language, and a choice of English, Hinglish or Hindi interface. If they can use WhatsApp, they can use this.

Is our data locked in?

No. Everything exports to Excel at any time, and your data lives in your own account. You stay because it works, not because you're trapped.
