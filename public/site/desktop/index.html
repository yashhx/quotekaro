Everything it does

Built from three years of walking into workshops.

Not a general business app with your trade's name pasted on it. Each of these exists because an owner in Faridabad or Wazirpur showed us the problem.

📥

Enquiries that log themselves

Paste a WhatsApp message and it pulls out the customer, the part, the quantity, the rate and the date they need it by. Connect your business number and they arrive on their own.

📊

A pipeline you can actually read

Every quote as pending, won or lost, with follow-up dates that turn red when they slip. One tap chases the customer on WhatsApp from your own number.

🧮

Costing on your real rates

Material, machine hour, labour, tooling, overhead, margin. The hourly rate is calculated from your depreciation, power and operator cost — not guessed.

⚙️

A live machine floor

Split a job across machines and watch each one's progress and finish time. Pause a machine, move the remaining pieces to another, and every estimate recalculates.

🚚

Trucks and yard stock

For traders: which vehicle went where, what the weighbridge said, and what the yard should be holding — with the shortfall flagged the same week.

Two-way

It reads TallyPrime. It does not disturb it.

A small connector on your Tally computer reads your ledgers, vouchers and bill-wise outstandings and puts them on your phone. It reads only — writing orders back into Tally exists, ships switched off, and stays off until you turn it on yourself. Validated against real TallyPrime Release 7.x on GST and non-GST books, reconciled to the rupee against Tally's own Bills Receivable report.

📄

Excel and PDF, both ways

Import your existing quote register, export whenever you want. Your data leaves as easily as it arrives — that is deliberate.

Honest about limits

What it does not do.

It does not predict machining time — you know your cycle time, the app does the money maths around it. Every finish-time estimate carries an 8% allowance for tool changes and breakdowns, and says so on screen. It does not replace Tally; it is the layer around Tally. And photo-based job matching is designed but not built yet, so it is not on this page as a feature.

The connector

Five minutes, three steps, and no Notepad.

The part every owner is nervous about, made as small as we could make it.

01

Copy a folder

Onto the computer that runs Tally. Nothing else is installed — it fetches what it needs itself.

02

Double-click, paste your key

It checks the key against your account and prints your shop's name back at you, so you know it is the right one.

03

Pick your sales ledger

It lists what is actually in your books and you choose by number. No spelling, no JSON, no exact-match errors.

After that it starts by itself every time the computer is switched on, with no black window on screen, and keeps a log you can read. Your accountant can watch it run for a week before you ever let it write anything.
