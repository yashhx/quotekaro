02 — MACHINE FLOOR

Which machine finishes when — without walking the floor.

Log a job once: part, quantity, cycle time per piece, and which machines run it. From then on the board shows live progress — percent done, pieces completed, time left and a realistic finish time — computed from plain clock time, on any phone, anywhere.

  • Split one job across machines — 200 pieces over two VMCs, each with its own clock.
  • Pause and transfer — a breakdown at 40%? Move the remaining pieces to a free machine; every estimate recomputes.
  • Honest ETAs — every estimate carries an 8% buffer for tool changes and small breakdowns, disclosed right on the card.
  • Handling time counted — loading, deburring, checking between pieces is part of the math, because 1 minute per piece moves a big batch's finish time by hours.

03 — TRUE COSTING

Price from your real numbers, not gut feel.

Underquoting loses money; overquoting loses the order. The costing wizard builds a price the way an estimator would — material, machine time, hand work, tooling — then adds your overhead and margin openly, so you can defend every rupee in front of a purchase manager.

  • A true hourly-rate calculator for each machine: depreciation, power, operator and maintenance. Most owners find their real rate is far higher than they assumed — the seeded VMC 850 example works out to ₹366/hr.
  • Built on the loaded-rate rule from a real shop visit: one operator running several machines? Enter his split salary per machine — hands-on minutes still cost the full labour rate.
  • Setup cost spreads over quantity — so small batches price higher per piece, the way they should.
  • A clean PDF quotation and a ready WhatsApp message, in under a minute.
You know your cycle times. TrackRakho never guesses them — it does the money math.

Straight answers

Questions owners actually ask.

Does it calculate my cycle time?

No — and be careful of anything that claims it can. You know your machines and your cycle times better than any software. TrackRakho takes the cycle time you enter and does everything after that: the money math, the machine ETAs, the tracking.

Do I need sensors or new hardware on my machines?

Nothing. The floor board works from plain clock time: you tell it when a job started and how long one piece takes, and it computes progress and finish times from there — with an honest 8% buffer for tool changes and small stoppages. Your machines stay untouched.

Will my accountant have to change how he works in Tally?

No. A small connector runs on the Tally PC. Won orders arrive as normal Sales Order vouchers he can see and edit; balances and open bills flow to your phone read-only. It is a window into Tally, not a replacement for it.

My customers demand quotes in Excel. Does that break this?

Not at all — that is exactly why the tracker exists. Keep quoting in whatever format your customer wants; logging that quote in TrackRakho takes under 30 seconds. You can also import your existing Excel sheet in one go, and export the whole pipeline back to Excel any time.

Is my data safe? Can I take it out?

Your rates, customers and quotes belong to you. Every shop's data is kept separate, it is never shown to any other shop, and the Excel/CSV export means you can take everything out whenever you want.

Which phones does it work on?

Any phone with a browser — Android or iPhone. It installs from the browser like an app (no app store needed) and the main screens are available in English, Hinglish and Hindi.
