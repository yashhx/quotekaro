A · Reaching a Tally partner
First touch

Cold, to a partner firm

After you find them on Tally's partner locator. Short on purpose.

Namaste [naam] ji, main Yash — Faridabad se. Maine aapko Tally ke partner locator par dekha. Main ek chhota app banata hoon jo TallyPrime ke saath chalta hai — shop owner ke phone par uske quotations, dispatches aur baaki payment dikhata hai. Tally ko sirf padhta hai, kuch likhta nahi. Aapke jaise partners ke liye ek programme rakha hai: pehle saal 30%, uske baad har renewal par 15%. 10 minute baat kar sakte hain? Main aapke office aa sakta hoon.
Warm — best odds

When a customer tells you who sold him Tally

Ask every customer: "Aapka Tally kisse liya?" Then send this. This converts far better than cold.

Namaste [naam] ji, main Yash. [Customer ka naam] ji ne aapka reference diya — unka Tally aapse hi hai. Wo mera app TrackRakho use kar rahe hain: Tally se unka bill-wise baaki paisa phone par aa jaata hai, aur quotations bhi track hote hain. Unhone kaha aapse baat karni chahiye. Aapke aur bhi clients ke liye kaam ka ho sakta hai — aur partner ko pehle saal 30%, phir har renewal par 15% milta hai. Kab time hai aapke paas?
Follow-up

Polite nudge after no reply

Once, after 4–5 days. Then stop — a second nudge annoys.

Namaste [naam] ji, upar wala message dekha hoga shayad. Koi jaldi nahi hai. Agar aapko theek lage to main 10 minute mein demo dikha deta hoon — aapke kisi ek client ke Tally par, unke apne numbers ke saath. Kuch install karne ki zaroorat nahi, sirf dekhna hai. Aap batayein, main uske hisaab se aa jaunga.
B · Partner introducing you to a shop owner

Send these to the partner so he can forward them. Written in his voice, not yours — that is why they work.

Machining

For a machine shop customer

[Shop owner ka naam] ji, ek cheez aapke kaam ki hai. TrackRakho — aapke Tally ke upar chalta hai. Phone par dikha dega: kaunsa quotation pending hai, kis party par kitna paisa kitne din se baaki hai, aur machine par kaunsa job kab tak khatam hoga. Tally mein kuch likhta nahi, sirf padhta hai. Inke founder Yash ji khud aa kar 20 minute mein dikha denge — aapke apne numbers par. Bhej doon unka number?
Scrap

For a scrap / metal trading customer

[Owner ka naam] ji, aapke liye ek cheez hai. TrackRakho — Tally ke saath chalta hai. Phone par: kis dealer par kitna paisa kitne din se baaki hai, kaunsi gaadi kahan gayi, aur yard mein kitna maal hona chahiye vs kitna nikla. Kanta check ka hisaab bhi rakhta hai — agar 1-2 tonne ka farak aa raha hai to usi hafte pata chal jaata hai. Tally mein kuch likhta nahi, sirf padhta hai. Demo 20 minute ka hai, founder khud aate hain.
C · Around the demo
Before

Confirming the visit

Namaste [naam] ji, kal [din, time] par aa raha hoon. 20 minute lagenge. Ek request: Tally wala computer chalu rakhiyega, company khuli ho. Us par aapke apne numbers dikha paunga — demo data se koi matlab nahi banta. Kuch install nahi karna hai. Bas dekhna hai.
The objection

When the accountant says no to Tally access

This will happen. Do not argue — agree with him, then narrow the ask.

Bilkul sahi soch rahe hain aap — books se zyada zaroori kuch nahi hai. Isiliye connector shuruaat mein sirf PADHTA hai. Tally mein ek voucher tak nahi banata. Ye setting aap khud dekh sakte hain, aur band karna ho to ek click mein band ho jaata hai. Jab tak aap kahenge nahi, likhne wala hissa chalu hi nahi hoga. Ek hafte chala kar dekh lijiye. Kuch bhi badla nahi to aage baat karenge.
The price question

When he asks why ₹999 when Tally is cheaper

Never defend the price against Tally. Move the comparison.

Tally ka to koi jawab hi nahi — wo aapka hisaab likhta hai, jo ho chuka hai uska. Ye uske aage ka kaam karta hai: jo paisa aana baaki hai, jo quotation bhoola pada hai, jo gaadi bahar hai. Muneem ka kaam yaad rakhne ka hai — ye usko yaad dilata hai. Saal ka ₹9,999 hai, matlab ₹27 roz. Ek bhoola hua quotation isse kai guna mehenga padta hai. Aap pehle dekh lijiye. Pasand aaye to baat karenge.
After

Same evening, after the demo

Namaste [naam] ji, aaj time dene ke liye dhanyavaad. Jo screen aapne dekhi — usmein aapka ₹[amount] abhi baahar ghoom raha hai, aur [X] bill 60 din se upar hain. Wo screen aapke phone par roz rahegi. ₹999 mahina, saal ka ₹9,999. Sab kuch isi mein — koi extra charge nahi. Koi jaldi nahi hai. Aap sochiye, main [din] ko ek baar poochh lunga.
