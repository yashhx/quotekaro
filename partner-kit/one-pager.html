The customer stays yours

  • He is tagged to your code, permanently. We do not approach your customers directly, and we do not put another partner on an account you opened.
  • You can charge your own fee on top. Onboarding, training, or folding it into your AMC - that money is yours and we take no part of it.
  • Nothing is written into his books. The connector reads TallyPrime only. Order push exists, ships switched off, and stays off until he turns it on himself. This is the objection his accountant will raise, and it is already answered.

What we ask of you

  • Only the introduction. Tell us which customers to meet. We run the demo and the install; you keep the relationship and the commission.
  • The first three demos, together. The founder comes with you. You learn the pitch by watching it, not by reading a deck.

Where the product stands - honestly

  • Early access. We are signing our first NCR customers now - you would be among the first partners, on the terms that go with being early.
  • The connector is tested on real TallyPrime - Release 7.x, GST and non-GST books, reconciled to the rupee against Tally's own Bills Receivable report.
  • What it does not do: it never predicts machining time, and it does not replace Tally. It is the layer around Tally.
