Between TSS renewals there is nothing new to sell to an existing customer. TrackRakho is that something. It sits on top of the TallyPrime you already installed, puts your customer's quotations, dispatches and unpaid bills on his phone, and pays you on every sale and every renewal after it.

What your customer gets

Machine job shops

Quotation pipeline with follow-ups, true hourly-rate costing, a live machine-floor board, and receivables aged bill by bill from Tally.

Scrap & metal traders

Yard stock that reconciles against the weighbridge, a truck board, dealer-wise receivables, and a variance flag the week material goes missing.

Why the customer says yes

  • It runs on his own numbers. The connector reads his TallyPrime and shows him his own stuck money — not a slideshow.
  • It reads only. Nothing is written into his books. Order push exists, is off, and stays off until he switches it on himself. This is the objection his accountant will raise, and it is already answered.
  • Setup is minutes, not a project, and it then starts by itself whenever the PC is switched on, with no window on screen.
  • One price, everything in it. No per-user maths, no modules, all his firms included.

What we ask of you

  • Only the introduction. Tell us which customers to meet. We run the demo and the install; you keep the relationship and the commission.
  • The first three demos, together. The founder comes with you. You learn the pitch by watching it, not by reading a deck.

Where the product stands — honestly

  • Early access. We are signing our first NCR customers now — you would be among the first partners, on the terms that go with being early.
  • The connector is tested on real TallyPrime — Release 7.x, GST and non-GST books, reconciled to the rupee against Tally's own Bills Receivable report.
  • What it does not do: it never predicts machining time, and it does not replace Tally. It is the layer around Tally.
