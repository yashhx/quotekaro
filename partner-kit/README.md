# Partner kit — Phase-0 assets

Two things to have ready **before** you approach the Tally channel. Neither is
deployed; both are standalone files.

## one-pager.html
What a Tally partner reads in ninety seconds. Fits **one A4 page** — open it and
press Ctrl/Cmd + P → "Save as PDF" to get the file you send on WhatsApp.

Structure: the opening (nothing new to sell between TSS renewals) → what the
customer gets, per trade → what the partner earns (30% year one, 15% recurring,
with the 20-customer maths) → why the customer says yes → what we ask → an
honest statement of where the product stands.

Deliberately has **no fake social proof**. It says "early access, signing our
first NCR customers now" because that is true today. The credibility it leans on
is the Tally validation, which is real.

## whatsapp-kit.html
Nine ready messages, tap to copy, built to be used on a phone outside someone's
office. Three groups:

- **A — reaching a partner:** cold, warm (after a customer names who sold him
  Tally), and one polite follow-up.
- **B — partner introducing you:** written in the partner's voice, one for
  machining and one for scrap, so he can forward it as his own.
- **C — around the demo:** confirming the visit, the accountant's Tally-access
  objection, the "Tally is cheaper" price question, and the same-evening
  follow-up.

## Tone rules baked in

Polite register throughout — this trade runs on relationships and a pushy
message costs more than it earns. Never send twice in a row, never after 9 pm,
and **never raise interest or legal points with a scrap trader**: traders are
excluded from the MSMED delayed-payment provisions, so that argument is untrue
for them and only belongs in machining conversations.

## Before you use these

Your own plan puts the channel at Phase 1, after 20–30 direct customers with
retention. Partners sell proof, not promises — the first question is "aur kaun
use kar raha hai?". Build the partner list from Tally's public locator
(tallysolutions.com/partner-locator/, 1,257 partners, filter by city) and note
which partner sold Tally to each customer you sign. Approach them when you can
name customers in their own area.
