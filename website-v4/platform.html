The Tally connector

A small program on the same PC as TallyPrime. It speaks to Tally's own local gateway, pulls what you need, and sends it to your phone. Your data file is never uploaded and Tally never asks for a password.

  • Bill-wise outstandings — every open bill with its own due date, part payments and credit notes accounted for
  • Party balances — debtors and creditors, so both sides of the money show on your phone
  • Dispatches — recent sales and purchases with quantities, matched back to your orders
  • Optional write-back — won quotations can become Sales Orders, only after you turn it on
Setup is three steps: copy a folder to the Tally PC, double-click once, paste a key. It installs what it needs and asks two questions. After that it runs by itself when the PC starts, with no window on screen.

Tested against a real TallyPrime, not a mock

A full set of books was entered by hand — sales with credit periods, part receipts, an advance, a credit note — and every figure the connector produced was matched against Tally's own Bills Receivable report.

Where work actually arrives

Your business runs on WhatsApp. So does the app.

Enquiries in

Connect your business number and messages that arrive — text, photos, PDFs — appear in the app as enquiry cards. One tap turns any of them into a tracked quotation, with customer, part, quantity and rate already filled in.

Reminders out

Chasing goes back out the same way, from your own number, with the part and amount written for you. The tone is deliberately courteous — this trade runs on relationships, and a reminder that reads like a threat costs more than it collects.

Email works too: RFQs arriving in Gmail can be pulled into the same pipeline.

Optional · off by default

It can read handwriting.

A rate list written on a page, a photographed purchase order, a supplier's list in Hindi — switch on smart reading and the form fills itself from the photo. Get it wrong and an empty field is better than a wrong price, so the reader is built to leave a box blank rather than guess.

  • Off until you turn it on, and reversible any time
  • Phone numbers are stripped from text before it is sent for reading
  • Photos cannot be redacted — the app says so plainly before you enable it
  • Your rates, customers and quotations are never used for reading anything else

Trust

Your books are yours.

This product asks for access to the most sensitive thing a business owns. That deserves plain answers rather than a policy page nobody reads.

Is my Tally file uploaded anywhere?

No. The connector runs on your PC and sends only the figures the app shows — balances, open bills, dispatch lines. The company file never leaves the machine.

Can it change my books?

Not unless you switch order push on yourself. Out of the box it cannot write a single voucher.

What if I stop paying?

Export everything to Excel or CSV whenever you want, including on your last day. There is no lock-in built into the data.

What is still being built?

Searching your old delivered orders by photograph — showing what you charged the last time you made the same part — is in development and is not in the app today. We would rather tell you that than show it in a demo.
