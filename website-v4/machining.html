02 · The rate that lost money

Most shops quote from the last rate, not from their cost.

Depreciation, power, the operator, maintenance — divided by the hours the machine actually runs. Very few shops have that number, so the quote is a guess anchored to whatever was charged last time, and margin disappears one job at a time.

TrackRakho computes your true hourly rate first, then prices the job on it. A VMC 850 in an NCR shop works out near ₹366 an hour — usually more than the owner expected.

When one operator runs several machines, his salary is split across them and folded into the machine rate; the minutes he stands at one machine stay at full labour rate. That single distinction moves a price more than most people think.

Log the job once — part, quantity, cycle time, handling time — pick the machines it runs on, and the board keeps the arithmetic for you from that moment on.

The law changed in August 2026. Most owners do not know yet.

Under the MSMED Act a buyer must pay a registered micro or small supplier within 45 days — and within 15 days if there is no written agreement, which describes most job-work orders. After that, interest runs at three times the RBI bank rate, compounded monthly, whether or not anyone asks for it.

The 2026 amendment added fixed timelines, an online dispute portal and recovery through the District Collector. TrackRakho does not send legal notices. It does something quieter and more useful: it keeps the number in front of you, and gives you a courteous message that shares the fact without threatening anybody.
