The problem

Three questions that take a day to answer — and are usually never asked.

01 · Quotations

“Which enquiries are still open, and which one did nobody follow up?”

Rates go out on WhatsApp and in Excel. A week later nobody remembers who was quoted what, or who never replied. A forgotten quotation is a ₹30,000 order that quietly went to somebody else.

02 · Receivables

“How much money is stuck, with whom, and for how many days?”

The answer exists inside Tally, bill by bill. It is on a desktop in another room, in a report nobody opens, in a form nobody can read on a phone at a customer's gate.

03 · Delivery

“Did everything that left the gate come back as payment?”

Material goes out on trucks and job cards. Payment comes back in parts, weeks later, against bill numbers nobody is matching. The gap is real money, and it is invisible until an audit or a bad month.

The answer

One screen that already knows.

TrackRakho reads what is already in your books and puts the three answers on your phone — no new data entry, no retyping, no new discipline to maintain.

  • Every quotation with its status and follow-up date
  • Every unpaid bill, aged from its own due date
  • Every dispatch matched back to the order it belongs to

Works on any Android or iPhone. Installs from the browser — there is nothing to download from a store.

The Tally connection

It reads your Tally. It writes nothing until you allow it.

A small program runs on the same PC as TallyPrime and carries data one way — out. Balances, bill-wise outstandings and dispatches come to your phone. Nothing is created, altered or deleted in your books. Order push exists, it is off, and it stays off until you switch it on yourself.

  • Read-only by default — verifiable in one setting
  • Runs on your PC — no Tally password, no cloud upload of your data file
  • Stop it any time; the app keeps working with what it already has
