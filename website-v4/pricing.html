Questions people actually ask

Before you decide

Do I need Tally for this to be useful?

No. Without Tally you still get the full quotation pipeline, follow-ups, costing, PDF quotations, the floor or yard tools and analytics. Tally adds the receivables side — who owes you, bill by bill.

My accountant will not allow software near Tally.

That is a reasonable position, and the reason the connector starts read-only. Show him that it cannot write anything; let him watch it run for a week. Order push stays off until he is comfortable, and it is his switch, not ours.

Tally itself costs less than this.

It does — Tally is a one-time licence with a small yearly renewal, and it is worth every rupee. TrackRakho is not competing with it. Tally records what has already happened; this tracks what is about to, and chases what has not been paid. The closer comparison is the person you pay to follow up.

How long does setup take?

The app itself, minutes — sign in and start logging quotes. The Tally connector is about five minutes on the accountant's PC: copy a folder, double-click once, paste a key.

Can I get my data out?

Any time, to Excel or CSV, without asking anybody. That is deliberate.
