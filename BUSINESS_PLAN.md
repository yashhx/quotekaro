## Channel playbook: Tally partners + accountant layer (researched 2026-07-30, wf_d9f9c41e)

**How the Tally partner channel works:** 28,000+ partners (3-Star/5-Star tiers). They earn
from: new-license margin, TSS renewals (Rs 4,500-13,500/yr - THE annuity), AMC, TDL
customization, cloud hosting, and 3rd-party product commissions. Between TSS renewals they
have nothing new to sell - that is the opening. NOTE: TallyShop is TDL-only; companion
SaaS (Biz Analyst/CredFlow/Livekeeping - and us) is sold OUTSIDE it via partner programs.
There is NO formal "Tally Integrated" certification - don't chase one.

**Proven programs to copy:**
- Biz Analyst: 1,000+ partners, pitch = BUNDLE with existing services; 4 personas (Tally
  partner / CA / consultant / hardware vendor); ran its own partner award tiers (status).
- CredFlow's mechanical flow: partner signs up > installs at customer using partner
  code/link > activates on Tally sync > auto-attributed earnings. Copy this exactly.
- Vyapar (most aggressive): up to 50% rev share, WEEKLY bank payouts, partner app,
  quarterly zone awards. Targets the tax-professional layer, not ICAI CAs.
- ClearTax: 15% share + WhatsApp-forwardable referral kit. Zoho: pays CAs in free
  software, not cash. RazorpayX: bought ICAI legitimacy (free year via bs.icai.org).

**The ICAI ethics fork (critical):** practicing CAs CANNOT take commissions (CA Act,
First Schedule; ESB rulings). So: pay CAs in VALUE (free product for their practice,
cleaner client books - bill-wise discipline makes filing easier, client discounts,
early-advisor status). Pay the layer BELOW in CASH: GST practitioners (1.3L+ registered),
munims, part-time accountants who visit shops weekly - Vyapar's 50%/weekly-payout
program targets exactly them. The munim who opens the shop's Tally every Tuesday is
the highest-leverage single human in our channel.

**Our sequence:**
- Phase 0 (now): 20-30 direct customers + retention proof. Partners sell proof, not promises.
- Phase 1 (first 5 partners, NCR): ask each early customer "aapka Tally kisse liya?" -
  recruit THAT partner (warm, trusted). Offer 30% year-1 + 15% recurring on renewals
  (beats Tally Anchors' 10/5 floor), founder joins first 3 demos, partner code +
  WhatsApp kit. Partner pitch: "TSS renewal ke beech bechne ko kuch naya nahi hai -
  ye aapke har client ka daily-use app hai, har saal renewal commission."
- Phase 2: munim/GSTP layer at Rs 500-1,000 cash per close; CAs via value not cash.
- Phase 3: tax bar association sponsorships (AIFTP: 138 district associations),
  Biz Analyst-style partner awards, partner dashboard.
- Product prerequisites before channel push: referral/partner code at signup
  (attribution), partner one-pager, WhatsApp demo kit.
