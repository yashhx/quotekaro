Collections, without burning relationships

The bill is late.
The message can still be respectful.

Your app reads the bills receivable record from Tally. It tells you how much is pending, which bill it belongs to, and how late it is. You decide what to say next.

  • ✓Bill-wise detail, not one big balance. “Bill 147, ₹95,500, 33 days late” is easier to collect than “aapka paisa pending hai.”
  • ✓Polite reminder, in your WhatsApp. It shares the context and asks respectfully; it never sends an automatic threat.
  • ✓MSMED byaj is opt-in and careful. It is an estimate, only for eligible Udyam-registered manufacturer/service MSE invoices.
