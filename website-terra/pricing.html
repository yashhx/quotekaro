Common questions

Will I have to stop using Tally?

No. Tally remains your accounting system. The connector starts read-only and brings selected reporting context into TrackRakho.

Does TrackRakho send messages without my approval?

No. A follow-up template opens in your own WhatsApp for you to edit and send. You stay in control of the customer relationship and the wording.

Can I use it for more than one company?

Yes. The single plan includes your companies while keeping each firm’s data and setup distinct.

Is the MSMED interest view for every business?

No. It is shown only for eligible Udyam-registered manufacturing and service MSEs, and it is marked as a conservative estimate. Pure trading businesses are not shown that legal feature.
