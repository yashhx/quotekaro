## The macro line (credibility opener, use ONE of these)
- Rs 8.1 lakh crore of MSME money stuck in delayed payments - Economic Survey 2025-26 (tabled Jan 2026).
- Peak was Rs 10.7 lakh crore (5.9% of GVA) in FY21 - GAME + Dun & Bradstreet; Rs 7.34 lakh crore by Mar 2024.
- ~80% of that money is owed to MICRO and SMALL units - the smaller the shop, the worse.

## The shop-level money map (Rs 1.5 crore turnover shop = Rs 12.5 lakh sales/month)
Bank's own rule (RBI/Nayak committee): a shop this size needs ~25% of turnover cycling as
working capital = Rs 37.5 lakh permanently outside the bank account. Where it sits:

| Where | Days | Rupees (Rs 1.5cr shop) | Can we touch it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| RECEIVABLES (bills raised, not paid) | ~73-day actual national avg (45 is the legal cap) | ~Rs 25-31 lakh | YES - core product |
| Raw material + WIP + FG (steel bought on 15-30d credit, sold on 45-90d) | 25-40 days | ~Rs 10-17 lakh | Partially (floor visibility) |
| Blocked GST input credit | +38 days to cash cycle; 21.4% of current assets (Bengaluru SME study, n=328) | Rs 3-8 lakh | NO - be honest |
| Retention/security (5-10% per bill, works contracts) | till defect-liability ends, interest-free | order-dependent | Visible in bill-wise aging only |
| Tooling/fixtures amortized per piece | recovered only if order runs full life | Rs 1-10 lakh lumps | NO |

Computed cash cycle from sourced norms: RM 30d + WIP 15d + FG 15d + receivables 75d - payables 20d
= ~115 days. A THIRD OF THE YEAR'S SALES is always outside the account.

## Why it stays stuck (this is the pitch)
1. **NOT credit policy - discipline.** 82.6% of invoices are written at 0-30 day terms, yet
   average payment takes 73 days (Recordent 2026, ~1.1 lakh MSMEs). The gap is follow-up, not terms.
2. **Admin leaks:** 61% of late payments trace to administrative errors / invoice reaching late
   (Sage); 49% of invoice disputes = wrong/missing PO reference (Quadient); 11% of buyers claim
   they "never received the invoice". Chasing WITH the bill number + reference kills these excuses.
3. **The law is theater at this scale.** MSMED Act: 45-day cap + 3x-RBI-rate compound interest -
   almost never claimed. MSME Samadhaan: 2.57 lakh complaints since 2017, only 9.4% mutually
   settled; owners don't file because the buyer stops giving orders (Economic Survey says this
   verbatim). So the ONLY lever a small shop really has = polite, persistent, SPECIFIC follow-up.
4. **Power asymmetry:** micro units wait up to 3x longer than bigger firms (median 195 days PAST
   the 45-day limit = ~8 months, GAME/CMIE 2021). Govt buyers worst (69% pay 60+ days late).
   NCR anchor: Maruti tier-1 settles T+45-60 from GRN; the squeeze lands on tier-2/3 job-shops.

## What the stuck money COSTS the owner (the closing line)
- Financed at bank OD 8-13% best case; NBFC 16-28%; informal 3-5% PER MONTH (many micro units).
- Rs 30 lakh stuck at even 15% = Rs 4.5 lakh/year of interest the owner eats silently.
- Treelife worked example: sliding from Net-30 to Net-90 on Rs 10cr revenue locks Rs 1.6cr extra
  = ~Rs 19 lakh/yr interest.
- GAME: MSMEs pay ~Rs 2 lakh crore of GST on revenue they have not received yet.

## Evidence that follow-up discipline works (our product claim)
- Businesses using AR/reminder software are 3x more likely to be paid before due date (Chaser 2026);
  92% of businesses get paid late without it.
- Biz Analyst (Tally companion, 50k+ businesses) claims up to 50% reduction in payment delays via
  bill-wise WhatsApp reminders - the direct comparable to our aging bar + bill drill-down.
- WhatsApp reminders: ~98% open rate vs ~20% email (AiSensy; vendor-claimed, use with care).
- GAME owner interview (verbatim): "I have divided my buyers on the basis of how many days they
  usually pay in... Knowing when a buyer usually pays helps me manage my cash flows" - an owner
  running our product IN HIS HEAD. That mental ledger is what we sell as software.

## Honest boundaries (never overclaim)
- We do NOT unblock GST credit, release retention, or change buyer terms.
- We make the stuck money VISIBLE (aging, bill-wise, per-party) and the follow-up SYSTEMATIC
  (due dates, WhatsApp chase with bill number). That is the whole - and defensible - claim.
- 43B(h) caution: pushing the 45-day law in a pitch can scare owners (buyers cancelled MSME orders
  over it; CMAI: Rs 5,000-7,000 crore lost in Q4 FY24). Sell follow-up discipline, not litigation.

## How often clients quote / receive RFQs (researched 2026-07-29)
Three client types, three totally different rhythms - QUALIFY BEFORE PITCHING:
1. **Approved-vendor shop (auto tier-2/3)**: almost NO RFQs - new RFQ only per new part/
   program (programs run 5-7 yrs; Maruti e-Nagare sends schedules, not enquiries). ~36-39%
   of small manufacturers are subcontractors with an "assured market" (NSS/ISID). For them
   pitch FLOOR + TALLY + payments, not pipeline.
2. **Open job-shop / general engineering (core Faridabad target)**: a handful of serious
   quotes per week (5-15/month) + repeat POs needing no quote. 90%+ of enquiry flow is
   WhatsApp/phone/referral - never touches a marketplace. US benchmark: small shop ~1
   quote/day, $5M shop ~60/month at ~25% win rate.
3. **IndiaMART payer / trader**: plan-capped 10-50 leads/week (Silver 10/wk), each lead
   shared with up to 6 competitors; quotes most, wins least; FIRST CREDIBLE QUOTE USUALLY
   WINS - speed is the weapon.
Win rates: typical 25-50%, falls as reach widens; best shops book ~70% of quotes vs 51%
average (Paperless Parts platform data, US). NO Indian survey of quotes/week exists -
genuine data gap; ASK EVERY SHOP VISITED ("hafte mein kitne quotation bhejte ho?") and
build our own dataset. Demo qualifier: if answer is "sab repeat schedule hai" -> pitch
floor/Tally; if "roz 2-3 enquiry aati hai" -> pitch pipeline + 30-sec log + follow-ups.

## Market size: how many machine-shop clients exist (triangulated 2026-07-29)
Three independent legs that converge:
1. **Registered factories** (ASI 2022-23): 2.53 lakh factories nationally; metal complex
   (basic metal + fabricated metal + machinery + auto, ~25%) -> **~60-65k registered
   metal/machinery/auto factories**.
2. **CNC installed base** (derived from IMTMA consumption: metal-cutting ~Rs 18-19k cr/yr,
   80%+ CNC by value, ~Rs 15-20 lakh/machine, 12-15 yr life): ~1.5-2 lakh working CNC
   machines -> at 2.5-4 machines/shop -> **~40-80k CNC-owning shops**.
3. **Cluster bottom-up**: Faridabad 12,015 MSME units, two-thirds in auto parts/sheet
   metal/fabrication (SIDBI cluster profile) -> ~8k metal units; NCR ~25-30k; ~15 major
   clusters -> broad universe **1.5-2.5 lakh metalworking workshops**.
CONVERGED: **core serious machine shops (CNC/factory-grade, 5-50 workers, the demo persona)
~50-70k in India; NCR ~5-8k; Faridabad ~2-3k.** Broad shell incl. conventional-lathe/
welding units: 1.5-2.5 lakh (Udyam's fabricated-metal+machinery are top-5 sectors of
5.7 crore registrations, but overwhelmingly micro). Within the core, current
software-payers ~15-25% -> ~8-17k shops - consistent with the 7-10k derived from the
5-7%-of-all-MSMEs-pay filter. Faridabad alone has enough core shops (~2-3k) to prove
the whole business without leaving NCR.
